# UI/UX

Answer the player's current question beside the relevant action: where to act, what changed, or what to choose. Give each screen a clear primary action. Use actual item previews, meaningful icons, short action labels, and visible selected/available states. Reveal detailed rules in contextual help or expandable sections. Let a successful action advance the introduction.

Follow Storm-Race's distinction between previewing and equipping, and cavy-cottage's contextual disclosure for rich systems. Judge themed controls at the ordinary camera and viewport, as in Dragon River Xiangqi. Keep action costs, rewards, legal moves, and the active turn consistent with game state.

Inspect entry, HUD, pause, results, and relevant inventory or shop through the normal journey. Preserve accessible names, readable contrast, keyboard navigation, focus return, comfortable touch targets, and reduced motion. Pair essential color signals with shape, labels, or position.

Finish when a first-time player can discover and complete the core action, understand its result, and continue. Record observed behavior and the actual source of feedback.
